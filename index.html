Before you book

Can we travel as a group of up to 8?

Yes. There are 8 passenger seats in addition to Shlomi’s driving seat. If a guide travels with you, they count as one of the passengers.

What about suitcases and special requirements?

Send the number and size of your bags, plus details of strollers or bulky items, so Shlomi can confirm the luggage arrangements. Ask about any child-seat or accessibility needs before confirming the booking.

How do I get a price and confirm a journey?

Call or WhatsApp +972 54 295 5555 with your date, pickup, destination or itinerary, passenger count and luggage details. For airport pickup, include flight details. Shlomi will discuss availability and a quote; your inquiry is not a confirmed booking.

What kind of driver will we meet?

Shlomi is a non-smoking, polite and professional driver who communicates in Hebrew and English. He is happy to chat when you want company and respects your preference for a quiet journey.
